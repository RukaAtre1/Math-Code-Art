# Recordings

The recordings are not distributed with the code. Put one file per piece in this folder; the renderer matches them by these filename patterns:

| Artwork | Pattern | Music | Recording | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lunar Drift | `*Clair_de_lune*.ogg` | Claude Debussy, Clair de Lune | Laurens Goedhart | CC BY 3.0 |
| Crystal Bloom | `*Dance_of_the_Sugar_Plum_Fairy*.ogg` | Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky, Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairy | Kevin MacLeod ([Free Music Archive](http://freemusicarchive.org/music/Kevin_MacLeod/Classical_Sampler/Dance_of_the_Sugar_Plum_Fairy)) | CC BY 3.0 |
| Mountain Current | `*mountain-spring*.mp3` | Mountain Spring (Full Version) | kaazoom (Pixabay) | Pixabay Content License |
| Dawn of an Orbit | `*Also_Sprach_Zarathustra*.ogg` | Richard Strauss, Also sprach Zarathustra: Introduction / Sunrise | Kevin MacLeod | CC BY 3.0 |
| Celestial Waltz | `*The_Blue_Danube*.ogg` | Johann Strauss II, The Blue Danube | not documented | CC0 1.0 / Public Domain dedication |

Any other recording also works with `--audio <file>`; without a curated piece, `--family auto` borrows the family of the most similar exhibition recording.
