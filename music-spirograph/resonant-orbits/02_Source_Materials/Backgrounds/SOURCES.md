## Art direction

- **Lunar Drift:** an unobstructed full moon against a quiet dark sky. Its cool silver-blue surface stays dimmer than the mathematical lines; the main drawing sits to its right.
- **Crystal Bloom:** a recognizable traditional wooden soldier occupies the left; the primary mathematical drawing is placed on the right. The evergreen and red/gold ornaments remain visible, with a local dark veil behind the drawing.
- **Mountain Current:** a bamboo forest supplies vertical rhythm and depth. Reduced saturation, softened foliage, and a local dark veil keep the central drawing prominent. The photograph was taken at Wakayama Farm in Utsunomiya, Japan; it is selected for atmosphere, not presented as a Chinese location.
- **Dawn of an Orbit:** real rays from an orbital sunrise supply the dramatic emergence requested in the reference. This is NASA photograph ISS063-E-58598, not a movie still. The sun and atmospheric rim remain visible.
- **Celestial Waltz:** the actual Danube at Budapest, with the Chain Bridge and Parliament. It is credited as Budapest, not misidentified as Vienna. The drawing occupies the sky above the riverside architecture.

## Provenance and use

[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) permits commercial and non-commercial use and modification. It excludes selling images without significant modification. Here the photographs are backgrounds to prominent, independently generated music-driven geometry; source photographs should not be sold separately.

[NASA image/media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) apply to the sunrise photograph. Credit NASA as the source of the underlying photograph; do not imply NASA endorsement. The selected crop does not feature identifiable people or NASA logos. The composited artwork is produced by this project and is not a NASA artwork.

The downloaded files are preserved at their native sizes. Each `.jpg.json` stores the exact source/download URLs, SHA-256, source dimensions, and compositing settings. Final 6000 × 3375 artworks render the mathematical geometry natively at that size; background photos are cropped and resampled to fill the canvas, so final pixel dimensions are not a claim that every photograph was originally 6000 pixels wide.

The former procedural `.png` files and their `.json` records remain as a fallback. Their credits are kept separate from the new `.jpg.json` records. `candidates/` contains considered alternatives and is not used by the runtime renderer.
